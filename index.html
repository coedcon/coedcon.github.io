Computing on Encrypted Data Summit in the Age of AI


October 16, 2026

Faculty Club, Leuven


On October 16th, we are hosting a one-day event dedicated to the latest advances in secure computation technologies. These technologies enable organizations to analyze and collaborate on sensitive data without exposing it, even in untrusted environments.

The program covers advances in secure computing techniques, with a focus on MPC, FHE, , and hardware-accelerated implementations, spotlighting real deployments and the path to wide adoption.

Theme: Secure AI on Encrypted Data: AI is transforming every industry, but its success depends on trust. This year's summit focuses on the technologies that make AI possible without compromising sensitive data. Among other topics, we will explore the latest advances enabling organizations to build, deploy, and collaborate on AI while preserving privacy and confidentiality.

Together, MPC and FHE are opening new opportunities for privacy-preserving data sharing, regulatory-compliant collaboration, and innovative digital services. From finance and healthcare to telecommunications and cloud computing, these technologies are redefining how industries can unlock value from data while keeping it secure.

We plan to invite leading researchers and practitioners, creating excellent opportunities for networking and collaboration.

Space is limited, so we will prioritize attendance for representatives from companies that are most likely to benefit from the event.
